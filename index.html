The honest answers

The questions people ask before they trust an agency.

Still unsure? Ask us in person. The floor is open and the kettle works.

Start a project
What is actually inside the retainer?

A written scope, before anything starts. What gets made each month, how often it goes out, which platforms it runs on, and who on the team is doing it. If a line is not written down, it is not in the plan.

Am I locked into a long contract?

Retainers run month to month with a notice period agreed up front. Ask about the notice period on the first call, and get the answer in writing before you sign.

How long before I see anything?

Content starts going out in the first month. Paid campaigns need roughly ninety days of real spend before the numbers mean anything. Anyone promising faster than that is guessing.

Who owns my ad accounts and my content?

You do. Accounts are set up in your name, and the files we shoot are yours. If you ever leave, you leave with all of it.

Will I meet the people doing the work?

Yes. Come to Damas Tower and meet the floor. The person planning your campaign sits near the person holding the camera, and you can talk to both.

Do you only work with big brands?

No. A single shoot starts at AED 599 and retainers start at AED 4,999 a month. Tell us the budget you actually have and we will tell you plainly what it buys.
