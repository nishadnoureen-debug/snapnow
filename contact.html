FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Quick answers about meeting our team, starting a project, and working with SnapNow in Dubai.

Where is SnapNow located in Dubai?

Our main Dubai office is situated in Damas Tower on Al Maktoum Road, Riggat Al Buteen, Deira. We are centrally located near Dubai Creek and 5 minutes from Union Metro Station.

How quickly can we start our project or retainer?

Once you reach out via our contact form or WhatsApp (058 551 1617), we hold a discovery call within 24 hours. Once the proposal is signed, our creative crew can onboard and begin production within 2–4 business days.

Can we schedule an in-person meeting at your office?

Yes! We welcome clients to our Damas Tower office for discovery sessions, brand workshops, and storyboard reviews. Drop us a message or call 058 551 1617 to book a dedicated time.

Do you cover shoots and campaigns outside Dubai?

Absolutely. We routinely shoot across all 7 Emirates (Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Ras Al Khaimah, Ajman, Fujairah, and Umm Al Quwain), as well as international productions across the GCC region.
